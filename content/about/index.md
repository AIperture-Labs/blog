This is the development blog of [AetherEngine](https://github.com/AIperture-Labs/AetherEngine),
a lightweight, modular game engine written in C++23 on Vulkan by
[AIperture Labs](https://github.com/AIperture-Labs).

The posts follow the engine as it is designed and built: the decisions, the
trade-offs behind them, and the code that came out of them. They go into
detail on purpose; each one should be useful on its own to someone building
an engine.

The engine is developed in the open. Issues and pull requests are welcome on
[GitHub](https://github.com/AIperture-Labs/AetherEngine/issues), and the
sources of this blog are in
[AIperture-Labs/blog](https://github.com/AIperture-Labs/blog).
